Patient records and visit documentation

MedicalSpace brings the patient’s history and each consultation into one workflow. Review earlier visits, document the current assessment, and keep the next steps in context.

Updated 26 November 2026 · Product guide by MANA LLC

Get it on Google Play
How to use itInside the applicationQuestions about this feature

Review the patient’s history

The patient record presents multiple dated visits. Open the relevant consultation to see the information recorded at that time. A history supports continuity, but the doctor still verifies that previous information is accurate and relevant to today’s assessment.

Document the consultation in context

Record the complaint, diagnosis, treatment plan and notes within the visit. Prescriptions and recorded vital signs can be reviewed alongside that consultation. Before finalizing, check the patient, dates, clinical text and medication details. If you use voice input, review the resulting text rather than assuming every word was transcribed correctly.

How to use it

  1. Open the patient record and review the dated visit list.
  2. Open or create the relevant visit and document the consultation.
  3. Review prescriptions, measurements and the follow-up plan before finalizing.

The doctor remains responsible for the accuracy, confidentiality and clinical interpretation of recorded information.

Inside the application

Original app captures. Patient records and the clinical chat are fictional examples; private contact details are excluded.

Questions about this feature

Can a patient have multiple visits?

Yes. The patient history shown in the app includes several dated consultations for the same patient.

Can I review prescriptions and vital signs with the visit?

Yes. The demonstrated workflows include prescription details and recorded vital signs in the context of the clinical visit.

Are the illustrated patient records real?

The records are fictional examples prepared for the website. The screenshots show the actual application interface without exposing a real patient’s medical history.
