Who is MedicalSpace designed for?+

MedicalSpace is designed for doctors and the team members they authorize to help with patient management and daily clinical workflows. It also brings professional networking and educational case discussions into the same app.

Can my team work with more than one doctor?+

Yes. A member linked to more than one doctor selects a doctor before accessing their patients. Access depends on the permissions assigned to that member and the features available to the doctor's account.

Which devices and languages are supported?+

The current app is designed for Android. The app interface supports English, French, Turkish and Arabic. The screenshots on this website show the English interface.

Does the AI make medical decisions?+

No. AI features support information organization and documentation. A qualified healthcare professional remains responsible for reviewing outputs and making clinical decisions.
